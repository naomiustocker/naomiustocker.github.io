About

Naomi Stocker has recently received her Bachelor of Fine Arts in Painting with a concentration in Drawing from the Rhode Island School of Design in Providence, Rhode Island. Throughout her artistic journey she explores a range of mediums and techniques such as oil and acrylic painting, intaglio printmaking practices, collage, and charcoal and ink drawings. With each project she aims to visually communicate her perspective and understanding of the world and her own identity.
